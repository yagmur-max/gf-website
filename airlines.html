Travel Information

Airlines & Baggage

Flights are booked with our partner airlines as part of your package. Here's what to expect on baggage, golf clubs and seating — the exact carrier and schedule for your trip are confirmed at booking.

Hand luggage

Each traveller over 2 years old may bring one piece of hand luggage up to 5 kg, max 55×40×20 cm.

Checked baggage

Checked baggage isn't included by default. You can add up to 20 kg of checked baggage per traveller during booking — this is cheaper than paying for it at the airport. Children over 2 may also check in a stroller or car seat, but this counts toward the paid baggage allowance. For children under 2, a stroller or car seat plus 10 kg of baggage is included at no extra cost. Strollers and car seats must be checked in, not carried onto the plane.

Golf bags & excess baggage

Golf bags are treated as special baggage and must be booked in advance of departure — they can't be added at the airport. The maximum weight per golf bag is 15 kg. Any excess weight beyond what you've booked is paid directly at check-in.

Seating

Advance seat selection isn't available on these flights — seating is allocated at check-in.

Baggage rules can vary slightly by route and airline. We'll confirm the exact allowance for your flights when you book, and our phone booking line can help with golf bag add-ons.

About Golfery

Golfery is a golf travel specialist that brings flights, hotels and tee times together into a single, simple booking. We're operated by Arentour AB, a tour operator with more than 20 years of experience arranging travel.

Our strength is local: a team on the ground in Belek with direct relationships with hotels and golf clubs, so tee times are confirmed in writing and there are no surprises at check-in.

Every package we sell is covered by a registered travel guarantee, so your payment is protected if your trip is cancelled or interrupted due to the operator's insolvency.
