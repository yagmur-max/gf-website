General Terms & Conditions

These terms apply to package trips booked through Golfery, combining flights, accommodation and golf. Please read them alongside your booking confirmation.

Draft content for internal review — this page is a sample starting point. Final wording should be confirmed by legal counsel before publishing.

1. Booking & confirmation

A booking is confirmed once we've received your details and any required deposit or full payment. You'll receive a written confirmation by email summarising your flights, hotel, green fees and total price.

2. Prices & payment

Prices are per person based on double occupancy unless stated otherwise, and include the inclusions listed on your confirmation. You can pay online by card or by phone with one of our travel consultants. Full payment is normally due a set number of days before departure, as shown at checkout.

3. Changes to your booking

Changes to dates, names or room type may be possible depending on availability, and may carry an administration fee charged by us or by our suppliers (airlines, hotels).

4. Cancellation

Cancellation terms depend on how close to departure you cancel, and are set out at booking. We recommend taking out travel insurance to cover unexpected cancellations.

5. Our liability

We're responsible for making sure the services in your package are delivered as described. Our liability for individual services (e.g. a specific flight or hotel stay) follows the terms of the relevant supplier and applicable travel law.

6. Complaints & claims

If something isn't as expected during your trip, please raise it with the hotel or our local team as soon as possible so we can try to put it right. If you're not satisfied after your trip, you can submit a written complaint to our customer service team.

7. Travel guarantee

Package trips booked with us are covered by a registered travel guarantee, protecting your payment if your trip is cancelled or interrupted due to our insolvency. See our Travel Protection page for details.

This is a general summary and not a substitute for the full legal terms that will accompany your booking.

About Golfery

Golfery is a golf travel specialist that brings flights, hotels and tee times together into a single, simple booking. We're operated by Arentour AB, a tour operator with more than 20 years of experience arranging travel.

Our strength is local: a team on the ground in Belek with direct relationships with hotels and golf clubs, so tee times are confirmed in writing and there are no surprises at check-in.

Every package we sell is covered by a registered travel guarantee, so your payment is protected if your trip is cancelled or interrupted due to the operator's insolvency.
