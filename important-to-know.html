Travel Information

Important to Know

A few practical things worth knowing before you travel, specific to golf trips.

Draft content for internal review — this page is a sample starting point. Final wording should be confirmed by legal counsel before publishing.

Course maintenance & closures

Courses carry out routine maintenance to keep playing conditions up to standard, and conditions can vary with weather. A course may also close temporarily in poor weather (heavy rain, storms, drought, snow, frost) if play could damage the course or put players at risk. These closures are outside our control and aren't grounds for a refund.

Handicap requirements

Most courses apply a handicap limit — typically an official handicap of 36 for women and 28 for men. If you don't have an official handicap, contact us and we'll check whether a tee time can still be arranged.

Golf etiquette & dress code

Most courses apply a dress code: collarless T-shirts, jeans, trainers/gym shoes and very short shorts are typically not permitted. Soft spikes are usually required.

Golf buggies & carts

Buggy availability and requirements vary by course, and can be restricted during overseeding (see our Overseeding Schedule). We'll advise on buggy options when you book.

Pre-booked tee times & green fees

Where your package includes a set number of green fees, tee times are normally pre-booked and confirmed in writing ahead of your trip.

Unlimited golf packages

"Unlimited golf" packages let you play as often as course availability allows during your stay, subject to the course's own tee time system and any seasonal restrictions.

Transfers to the golf course

Unless stated otherwise, transfers between your hotel and the golf course aren't included in the package price and can be arranged locally or added at booking.

Specific rules can vary by hotel and course — we'll confirm anything relevant to your exact trip before you travel.

About Golfery

Golfery is a golf travel specialist that brings flights, hotels and tee times together into a single, simple booking. We're operated by Arentour AB, a tour operator with more than 20 years of experience arranging travel.

Our strength is local: a team on the ground in Belek with direct relationships with hotels and golf clubs, so tee times are confirmed in writing and there are no surprises at check-in.
